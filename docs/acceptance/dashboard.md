# Dashboard screen acceptance

`/dashboard` renders the generated `DashboardMetrics` report through `DataSource`. The UI formats values only; every metric, sample count, delta and coverage figure comes from the Rust report. No UI library, chart framework, dependency or storage was added; generated DTOs only mirror Rust report shapes.

## Layout

The Dashboard fits its window. The outlet is the page's viewport: the heading, the summary and the four tiles keep their height, and the two rows under them — Effort beside Environment, then Sessions — take the rest of the window in equal shares, except that Sessions is never taller than its own rows: a short list ends just under its rows and leaves the spare height to Effort and Environment, an opened group grows it, and once it reaches its share of the window its rows scroll inside the table (user decision, 2026-09-29). Each of those rows is as tall as its own content with its list at three rows, so at the native minimum (1120×720) the effort total, a plot of at least 50px and its axis, three environment identities and three lane rows are wholly in view, and a taller window (1280×800, the 1440×900 default and beyond) shows more rows rather than more page. Nothing is clipped to fit: the identity list and the lane table scroll inside themselves beyond what they show, as keyboard-focusable regions, every secondary detail opens over the page in the kit `Modal` without moving it, and the full history belongs to the Sessions view. Sessions is the last block: no measurement or index line follows it. Below the supported window size the outlet scrolls rather than a list losing rows; nothing in the page hides content with `overflow: hidden` alone.

The primary composition, top to bottom:

- The TopBar shows the report period immediately before the 7d/14d/30d presets. It updates with the range, is hidden while a range loads and off the Dashboard, and gives the time zone in its tooltip and accessible text.
- Heading "What your agents did", then sessions, human messages, tool calls and favorite model (M-10; an unknown favorite shows `—` with its reason).
- **Agent / human hours** and **Caught by your rules** are not shown (user decision, 2026-09-29). The page draws neither panel nor any of their values, daily bars, definitions or controls, keeps no row for them, and the four tiles follow the summary at the page's own gap; their height goes to the two flexible rows below. The report is unchanged: it still carries agent hours, the human estimate, the ratio, the daily hours and the rule-fire entry. `HeroDayBars.tsx`, `hero-days.ts` and `RollingValue.tsx` stay as components with their own unit tests but are not drawn on the Dashboard.
- Four StatTiles with full labels: Agent h/day (the note states the day divisor), Concurrency (the mean, with no visible unit, and the max beside it; its definition says "Displayed value is mean concurrency; max is the peak overlap." from hover, focus and keyboard alike), Merged PRs (M-19: the report's value only when every confirmed-linked pull request's cached facts decide it; otherwise `—` with the reason naming the known subtotal and the unknown count, and the aside `<known> + <unknown> unknown`, never `0`; the definition adds untyped merged pull requests and the refresh status), and Hands-off median (with p90). Each definition quotes its rule and adds the report note, reason, sample counts and suppressed-delta explanation. The hands-off definition begins "How long your agents run before they need you." and lists the surfaces in `hands_off_excluded_surfaces` with qualifying and degenerate session counts.
- **Effort** (M-19) and **Environment** (M-17), at 1.6:1. Both are layered as Sessions is: a 36px title row on the page's canvas — the kit header's ink and weight, the card's definition, then its controls — over the kit's inset surface panel (its 1px border, 9px radius and lift, 6px inside the card), which holds the whole body: for Effort the range's total and the chart, the merge markers with their date axis; for Environment the identities under their column header (whose `14 DAYS` cell is the strips' legend control) and the dialog controls, and its loading and failed states alike. The panel is a flex column, so only the lists inside it scroll. The four tiles stay flat surface cards, as the reference draws them. Environment is described below. The card is titled Effort, not by work type, and draws no work type: every session in the range counts, linked or not. Its definition adds, after the M-19 rule, that the card shows every session's total per day without splitting it by work type and that each session's hours count toward the model it used most. Effort draws the report's `pr_effort` section and computes nothing but where to draw it and how to say it (`effort-chart.ts`). Above the chart, on one 22px row, the range's total for the selected measure in large tabular figures ("655 agent h", or "$14,066+" with a "+" when any selected response in the range is unpriced, "cost unknown" when none could be priced), "last N days", and one short note when there is something to add: in cost, how many responses have no price; in agent hours, how many days are above 24 h (user decision, 2026-10-01). The chart is one single-colour bar per local day, in the accent colour, for that day's total of the selected measure over the whole cohort (`cohort.by_day`): no series per assignment, no stacking and no legend. Every day is on one shared scale from a zero baseline to a round value at or above the busiest day (1, 1.2, 1.6, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 or 8 times a power of ten, so its half is round too). A 36px gutter labels the scale at its top, middle and zero in the measure's unit with up to three significant digits: hours (`h`, or `min` and then `s` when the busiest day is under 0.1 h and under a minute, so a short range never reads `0 h`), or dollars ("k" from 1,000 dollars, and fractions of a cent such as 0.003 dollars below a cent); the top is round in the unit it is labelled in. In agent hours, once the busiest day reaches 12 h the scale reaches at least 24 h and a dashed reference line labelled "24 h" marks one full day of clock time (unlabelled when it is the top line, which the scale already names); agents running at the same time add up, so a day can pass it. A day with nothing to draw is an empty slot: no agent time, no selected response, or in cost a day where not one response could be priced ("cost unknown"), never a zero bar. A partly priced day is drawn at its priced subtotal with a small "+" above the bar. A range with sessions but no agent time says "No agent time in this range." in hours, as a range with nothing priced says "No priced daily usage to plot." in cost; a day with nothing priced reads "cost unknown" in its card. An "agent h / cost" segmented control switches the bars and the total between M-05 agent time and API-equivalent dollars from the same cohort, and keeps its choice across range changes; agent time stays the default. Dollars price the same selected responses the API-equivalent cost prices — every token of each response (fresh input, output, cache reads and cache writes, Claude writes split by five-minute and one-hour lifetime) at its own model's rate from the bundled catalog — on each response's event day, so the range's priced subtotal equals the cost tile's. A response that cannot be priced is never added as $0. Codex responses, which record no service tier, are priced at OpenAI's default tier and counted as assumed. Each day's column, the full height of the plot, is a tab stop whose accessible name carries the day, its total, its three largest models with their amounts and shares (and how many more), the unpriced responses by model, the above-24 h note and the merged numbers. Hovering or focusing a day opens its card (a passive Base UI tooltip beside the column): the date and total, then every model used that day, largest first, with its amount and share of the day ("<1%" for a tiny one); in cost, the unpriced responses by model ("No price: 128 codex-auto-review responses"); in agent hours above 24 h, "Above 24 h: agents ran at the same time". The models come from the report's per-day model split (`by_day[].models`), which adds up to the day exactly: a day's cost is split by each response's own model; a day's agent time is split by session, each session's time going to the model it used most in the range (the most selected responses, then the most output tokens), and a session with no response that names a model counts as "no model recorded". Under the baseline a marker lane puts one badge on each local merge day counting that day's pull requests, deduplicated per pull request, with each pull request's number, type, link confidence and refresh status in the badge's accessible name, and the gutter states the range's count; markers are not an allocation of effort. The date ticks (every day of a week, every other day of a fortnight, every fifth day of a month, counted back from the last), the markers and every bar are placed from one day-centre mapping. The total's row is 22px with 6px under it, so the scale's top label, centred on the plot's top line, stays clear of it, and the chart is at least 82px — a 50px plot and the 29px axis — and the plot takes all of the card's spare height. The card has no unresolved triangle: an unresolved assignment changes nothing it draws (the merged-PR tile states pull requests without cached facts). The static method is one dialog away from the card's header ("Details", opening "How effort is counted · daily values"): it explains the bars for the selected measure, the 24 h line, how a day's hours are split by model ("Each session's hours count toward the model it used most"), the markers and their confirmed links, how many selected responses could not be priced (each model and reason) and how many Codex responses were priced at an assumed default tier, then holds each day's agent hours, cost (a partial subtotal marked "+") and merged numbers as a table, scrolling inside a named keyboard-focusable region. There is no table by model. The Details dialog then holds the range's usage as two titled sections, each with its M-04 definition and scope beside its title: **Tokens per day** and **API-equivalent cost** (described under Supplementary measurements), after the daily values. Details is held by the page, so an open Details survives another range loading. After Details, the header carries a small red ! only when the pull-request checks need the user (user decision, 2026-10-05): a linked pull request's last check failed, automatic checks paused because gh is missing or signed out, or automatic checks are off (or the data source never checks on its own) and some pull request was never checked. Pull requests not checked yet while automatic checks run draw nothing. One function decides this (`prAttention` in `pr-effort.ts`); the Merged PRs tip points to the red ! only when that function says it is shown. The red ! is named "Pull-request checks need your attention", its tip says in plain words what is wrong ("2 pull requests could not be checked. Click to check them again."), and it opens the refresh dialog described in [DATA-SOURCE.md](../DATA-SOURCE.md#pull-requests), whose first line is the confirmed-linked refresh status that used to sit under the chart. While a refresh batch runs the mark stays, quiet rather than red and named "Refreshing pull-request facts…", so its progress and Cancel stay one click away. The card has no line under the chart.
- **Sessions**: one row per session active in the report's fixed last 48 hours, whatever range is selected, carrying every span the report returned for that session on that 48-hour axis, under the design's columns **session · PRs · started · activity · cost**. The host glyph sits with the session. The session cell names the row by its host's own title — read for the rows inside the table's scroll area and never stored ([session-titles.md](session-titles.md)) — or the title a source saved, else by enough of its real session ID to tell two rows apart, followed by the indexed repository and branch as compact meta; only the context gives way to the ellipsis first, and a long saved title ends in one only beyond three quarters of the cell. Screen readers get the title, the context, the full ID, the span count and times, the start, the recorded links and the cost, with how many responses have no published price, in the row's own description. The name is a link that opens that one session (`Open session [title, ]<id>`), carrying the whole canonical ID as the search, the reported host when the Sessions filter supports it and the selected range, so the detail page's Back link returns to a list that holds it; its focus ring is drawn inside the clipped name. **PRs** counts recorded session → pull request links from `lane_sessions.pr_links`: every evidence level, the whole session, not merge status; a grey evidence dot marks a count that includes inferred links, and the count's accessible text says how many are inferred. Its header is a keyboard-reachable definition (M-13) stating in plain words that these are pull requests linked to the whole session, merged or not, by exact, commit and inferred links, and that zero means no link is recorded, not that no pull request exists. An indexed session with no link reads `0`; a row with no context row reads `—` with its reason. **started** is the start the host recorded, or for a Claude session (Claude Code records none) its earliest imported message, in the report's zone (day and 24-hour time, with year and zone in the title), however long before the axis it was; an unknown start reads `—` with its reason and is never replaced by the first span. The activity column's visible name, "Activity", sits over its tick row inside the same 24px header band, so naming it adds no height. There is no span-count or first-seen column: the span count stays in the row's description. A right-aligned **cost** column carries the whole session's API-equivalent cost from `lane_sessions.cost`: the same selection and prices as the cost tile, over all of the session's history, including work before the 48 hours and before the selected range. A collapsed group's row instead shows its total, marked `Σ` (e.g. `Σ $7,922+`): the parent's own whole cost, when it has a row, plus every sub-session it opens to, at any depth — so the cost cells in view add up to the same total whether a group is open or not; open, every row shows its own. A listed session's group includes all of its verified sub-sessions, whenever they ran, not only those active in the 48 hours: the report carries the others in `lane_sub_sessions`, and opening the group lists each as its own row under its parent — its name, link, start, PRs and whole cost, with an empty activity track and a description saying it had no activity in the last 48 hours — so the disclosure's count is the rows it opens to. A group total is a floor (`+`) when any member has unpriced responses, which its title names by model and reason (a response with no recorded time is one: "no time recorded"), when a member's cost is unknown ("2 sessions' cost unknown, not included"), or when the report left sub-sessions out at its limit ("4 more sub-sessions not shown, not included", or "at least 4 …" when the report's walk was cut short); its title starts "Total for these N sessions". A row that shows no total of the sub-sessions left out under it — an open group, or a listed session none of whose sub-sessions is in view — says so beside its own cost and in its description ("4 more sub-sessions under this session are not shown or counted."). `$12.40` when every response was priced; `$12.40+` when some have no published price (the priced part only, a floor; its title says how many of how many were priced and names the rest, e.g. "980 of 1,000 responses priced; 20 codex-auto-review have no published price"); `—` with a reason when responses exist but none could be priced (the reason names each model and why, from `lane_sessions.cost.unpriced`, e.g. "codex-auto-review has no published price"), when the session (or every session in a group) recorded no response, or when no indexed session owns the row; a measured zero reads `$0.00`, a positive amount below a cent `<$0.01`, and from $1,000 whole dollars (`$1,234`) so the 80px column holds `Σ $12,345+`. Its header is the definition trigger (`Whole-session cost, definition`, M-04) and states in plain words that the number is what each whole session would cost at each model's public API rate, including work before the last 48 hours, and that a collapsed group shows Σ, the total of the session and every sub-session listed when it is opened; that `+`means some responses have no published price and`—` that nothing could be priced; and that Codex responses with no service tier recorded are priced at OpenAI's default (standard) tier. No caption follows the rows. Beside the card's title a short subtitle, "Active in the last 48 hours", says which sessions are listed; the session and span counts and the span cap are not stated on the card. The card's definition (M-05) says in everyday words that the card lists the sessions active in the last 48 hours, that every number in a row is about the whole session (started is the recorded start, for Claude Code the first message; PRs are linked pull requests, merged or not; cost is the whole session's), and that only the Activity bars stop at 48 hours. A verified sub-session (`lane_sessions.parent`, only as the report sends it) is listed under the session that created it, collapsed: a listed parent keeps its own row, measurements and link and gains a disclosure — a chevron and the count of the sub-sessions it opens to, a real button named `N sub-session(s) of <parent>`with`aria-expanded`, operable by Enter and Space, with its focus ring drawn inside the 22px row. A parent with no span returned in this report — the report caps its spans, so the parent may still have been active in the window — gets one synthetic row instead, at its newest returned child's place: the same disclosure, "Sub-sessions of" and a link to the parent's own page (`Open parent session <name>, <id>`), and blank PRs, started and activity cells — the row has no activity lane, cost, link count or start of its own, its description says the parent is not listed here, and its count is only of listed children. Its group lists its sub-sessions active in the 48 hours, each with all of its own sub-sessions; collapsed, its cost cell shows their `Σ`total; open, the cell is blank and each sub-session shows its own. Opening a group lists each child's own row, as it reads ungrouped — its host title, parent marker, link, metrics and track — indented under the parent, newest first; closed, the children are not in the page at all, so no hidden link takes focus and no title is read for them. A group, and each child within one, sits where its newest returned member does. A session whose parent is unknown, ambiguous or conflicting —`null` in the report — or whose parents would lead back to itself stays an ordinary row, unless the report marks it a known sub-session (`lane_sessions.known_child`): a known sub-session with no verified parent is not listed at all, and neither is any returned session whose verified parents lead to it, nor any returned session whose unreturned direct parent the report's context-only entry marks the same way. Such a session gets no row, no named-parent row, no link, no accessible text and no title, live-state or compaction read; this is decided before any row is drawn, so grouping cannot bring it back. An unreturned parent's own parent is not followed. A report without the bit, or with no context for a session, leaves it listed, and an ordinary session with no title, live status or parent is never a known sub-session. When every returned session is left out, the empty table says there is no session to list rather than no active span. The union is flat, drawn by the same table in the same 22px rows and columns, and the table sizes from the rows drawn, so opening a group grows the card up to its share of the window and scrolls inside it beyond that, closing it shrinks the card again, and the page never scrolls. The definition states that sub-sessions are listed under the session that started them, older ones included, that a collapsed group's cost, marked Σ, is the total of the session and every sub-session listed when it is opened, that an absent parent is only named, on a row with no bars or numbers of its own, whose group lists its sub-sessions active in the 48 hours with their own sub-sessions, and that a session recorded as created by another session that is not verified is not listed, nor its sub-sessions, and the Sessions page leaves them out the same way. Sessions groups its own lists by the same rule ([indexed-sessions.md](indexed-sessions.md)); each child is still its own session with its own route, search membership and measurements. The table shows at least three rows and scrolls inside itself beyond what it shows. "View sessions" opens the Sessions route with the same selected range.

Card titles carry no visible rule ID. Each titled card has one small info control beside its title, and each supplementary measurement has the same control beside its title inside its dialog: a real button named "<title> definition" whose Base UI definition quotes the rule (the ID is read there, first, as the name of the text it quotes); hover, focus and Escape behave as on every other definition. Untimed indexed history (`untimed_history`) is not a banner above the cards: when the report counts any, it is a titled section of the Coverage dialog — the full sentence, the all-history count ("N records · outside dated measurements") and the host/surface breakdown — and the Coverage control's accessible name ends with "N untimed records". It is never a share of anything and never shown when the count is zero. `e2e/design-alignment.spec.ts` checks, in light and dark at 1440×900, that no rule ID is visible text, that the two hidden panels have no definition control, and that every card's definition opens from keyboard focus and closes on Escape, including the three inside their dialogs; `e2e/untimed-disclosure.spec.ts` checks the control's height, wording and keyboard disclosure on both pages.

**Unresolved triangles.** A panel with unresolved data — Environment when `selected_unresolved_calls` or a positive `selected.unresolved` group exists — ends its header with one small warning-ink triangle, the last control of the header's end group, so it sits at the panel's top right. It replaces the orange in-body note and cohort suffix; a panel with nothing unresolved draws none. The triangle is a real button whose name carries the count ("Unresolved attribution: 38 unresolved observations, including untimed"). Hover and keyboard focus read the gist — the counts and, for Environment, each host and reason split into the selected range and untimed — in a passive Base UI tooltip that holds no control; click, tap, Enter or Space open the whole explanation in the Dashboard's detail dialog, which scrolls long content inside the window, closes on Escape and returns focus to the triangle.

Continuous values read one way across the page. Agent h/day, the concurrency mean, and the hands-off median and its p90 aside are formatted by the app-level `metric-format.ts` helper `continuous` — the one Sessions already uses — applied after any unit conversion the tile makes. The shared one-decimal scale rounds anything under half a tenth to `0`, which would read as "nothing happened" for a range that did a little; such a value renders `<0.1` instead, so the only `0` here is a measured zero and an unmeasured value still renders `—` with its reason, because a formatter is never reached for one. The scale, its one decimal and every value reaching it are unchanged, as are integer and token counts, percentages, cost, timestamps and chart geometry.

### Environment (M-17)

Environment reads its own `environment(days)` query (`queryKeys.environment`, refreshed by the shared `metrics` invalidation, including enrichment that adds no record). The Dashboard report's `unavailable.environment` entry is no longer shown. The panel formats values only. It never sums, re-sorts or ranks.

- **Summary.** No summary line is shown: the selected-range observed calls and identities (`totals`) are not stated as a line on the card. The card's header carries no inventory pill.
- **Unresolved attribution.** When `selected_unresolved_calls` is above zero, the header's unresolved triangle states that count on its own ("38 unresolved observations", plus "including untimed" when a `missing_timestamp` group exists) in its name and tooltip. It never shows it as "X of Y" or as a share of the observed calls. `selected_unresolved_calls` includes observations with no timestamp, which no window holds and `selected_calls` excludes, so any such fraction would be false. The tooltip and the dialog the triangle opens list each host and reason from `selected.unresolved` in two groups:
  - **In the last N d**: hook summary events (saved summaries, without proof of each hook's execution), unknown kind, unstated MCP detail and unstated skill name;
  - **Untimed**: `missing_timestamp` groups, stated as untimed observations that cannot be assigned to any selected range and are not part of the range's observed calls.
- **Top eight rows.** The first eight `identities`, in the order the report gives, in a bounded list that always shows three dense 24px rows, more when the card has room, scrolls inside the card beyond that and takes keyboard focus. One column header — `TOOL · KIND`, `14 DAYS`, `CALLS` — sits above the list, outside the rows that scroll and not counted among them, on the same three tracks the rows use (the calls track is as wide as the longest count shown, in the row font's own character width; header and list reserve the same scrollbar gutter), and describes the list for assistive technology (`aria-describedby`; the tool cell reads "Tool · kind · host" and the calls cell "Calls · last N d"). No table roles are put around the ordered list. Each one-line row shows, in that column order:
  - the tool: a truthful name — `server · tool` for MCP and the skill name for a skill, or the raw name with what was not stated (`kind not recorded`, `MCP server and tool not stated`, `skill name not stated`) — then the raw kind badge (an unknown kind reads `unknown kind`, and a future kind is shown verbatim), then the host as a muted suffix. The kind badge keeps its room; the host gives way first when the line is short, then the name truncates; the tool's title always holds the full name, note, kind and host with its surface;
  - the fixed 14-local-date `DayStrip` from `strip`, shaded at 0, under 3, under 8, and 8 or more;
  - selected-range calls, right-aligned at the row's edge.
    Hook summary rows read "Hook summary events" and count events, never calls of a named hook.
- **Compact row.** Effort and Environment stay at 1.6:1 and share a height: the window's share, plus whatever a short Sessions list leaves, never their own data's. Sessions stays in view at both sizes.
- **Range.** 7/14/30 changes the selected calls and totals, and the calls header's accessible text ("Calls · last N d"). The strips keep the same 14 dates. The `14 DAYS` column header keeps that fixed scope in view whatever range is selected, and is itself the control (accessible name "14 days · activity strips, fixed 14 local days") that opens an "Activity strips" dialog stating the exact dates, the zone, the shade thresholds with their three swatches (1, 3, 8+), and that this is observed use only, with installed components unknown. When no identity is observed there is no column header, and the same control keeps its own line ("Activity strips · fixed 14 local days") above the dialog controls. The sidebar is unchanged.
- **No inventory claims.** Installed components are unknown. The panel shows no used/installed ratio, no not-installed label and no never-called claim.
- **Dialogs.** The kit `Modal` (BaseUI Dialog) holds four dialogs, each opened from a compact control (the unresolved triangle, "Observed · N", "Configured · N" and the strips' legend in the `14 DAYS` header, named in full for assistive technology). Each is keyboard-operable, traps focus and returns focus to its control.
  - **Unresolved attribution**: as above.
  - **Observed identities · N** appears whenever any identity is observed. It lists every row in report order, in a focusable, scrollable list, each with its kind badge plus host and raw surface context, looked up in `selected.observed` (or `strip.observed` for a strip-only row), with `unknown surface` for `null`. When selected Claude hook summaries exist, opening this dialog also starts a bounded, cancellable read of their recorded original sources. “Names found in saved summaries” lists only approved script labels and the number of distinct checked summaries mentioning each one; it separately states how many selected summaries were checked, unavailable, and had unnamed commands. It does not count executions or match labels to configured components. Closing the dialog or changing range cancels the read.
  - **Configured components · N** lists `configured` (host, kind, name, source, scope and enablement), stating that being configured does not show that a component is installed, callable or called. It also lists per-source and root statuses. Plugin caches sit in a separate **Cache only** section. No path, command, URL or secret is displayed.
    - An empty `configured` list reads "No configured components were verified." When any source is incomplete, malformed, unreadable or unsupported, it adds that those sources were not fully read, so components may be configured that it does not show. It never says the supported files name no component.
    - An `entry_limit` count from a directory source (the Claude skills directory and both plugin caches) is a lower bound, because the listing stops one past the bound. It reads "at least N entries, at least M skipped … both are lower bounds". A file over the bound states its full count and keeps its exact figures.
- **States.** Loading, a safe error without backend text plus "Retry environment usage", and an empty observation. None of these hides the rest of the Dashboard.

Supplementary measurements open from the card they belong to, each in a dialog over the page whose header carries its title, its definition and its scope; there is no measurement line. Tokens per day and API-equivalent cost are titled sections of Effort's **Details**; **Coverage** is a one-word control in the Sessions card's header, beside its definition and "View sessions", whose accessible name adds the capture inventory's state when it is known and not complete, token measurement when it misses sessions or has gaps ("tokens measured for 2/3 sessions (incomplete token counters)"), a failed fixed 14-day gate ("trailing 14 days below the 90% gate") and the untimed record count when there is one ("Coverage: 2,318 untimed records"); a complete inventory with every session measured, no failed gate and no untimed history adds nothing. Both are held by the page, so an open one is kept while another range loads and reopens over the new report. Their content is unchanged:

- **Tokens per day** (M-04), captioned with the recorded total (or "total unmeasured") and `usage_coverage.total` measured/sessions: one row each for fresh input, output and cache read, each on its own scale labelled "measured peak" (a series with no numeric counter says "no recorded measurement"; a measured zero peak stays 0), with a table of every day's counters including cache writes and total. The row count matches the range (7, 14 or 30 days). A day without selected usage is "no recorded usage", a null counter is a dashed "unmeasured" bar, and a measured zero stays 0.
- **API-equivalent cost**: total, catalog version, `as_of`, basis, priced and unpriced response counts, and each unpriced model/tier with its reason. When `total_usd` is null, the total shows `—`; a priced subtotal is described as partial and never shown in the total slot.
- **Coverage**, with the inventory state beside its title unless it is unknown: token measurement (total and by surface, gaps named, fixed trailing-14-day gate); session capture with the inventory state (only `fresh_complete` uses the success tone; nothing checks the inventory yet, so `unknown` is not shown at all, neither as a pill, in the title line, in the control's name nor as a surface's "session inventory unknown" reason; an empty list reads "No session capture coverage rows available.", which does not prove there are no receipts), a note that historical receipts do not mean a surface is capturing now and that indexing does not certify capture coverage; hands-off timestamp-health exclusions; and, when the report counts any, **Untimed history** as above.

The native index's phase and freshness are the sidebar's status row and Settings'; the Dashboard adds no index line and states none of the index's diagnostic reasons.

Percentages in the report are percentage points (50 means 50%). `present.ts` divides by 100 exactly once before the kit fraction formatters. Suppressed deltas are not shown.

Every tile draws the same two rows whatever its values: the icon and the full label on the first, and on the second, across the tile's width and ending at its right edge, the value with its unit, change and aside. The value's text sets that row's baseline, so the four values — measured, unknown or dense — share one baseline and one right inset, and the tiles are as tall as a wrapped tile always was (63px).
