# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in XTrace Desktop's issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other project spaces should be treated with respect. Discuss ideas and code
constructively, welcome questions, and respect people's identities and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual attention, and disclosure of
someone's private information without consent are unacceptable. Do not retaliate
against someone for raising a concern.

Report concerns privately to a project maintainer through an established private
channel. If you do not have one, ask a maintainer how to contact them privately
without posting the incident details or identifying the affected people. If a
maintainer is involved, contact another maintainer where one is available.

Maintainers will consider reports in context, restrict disclosure to people
needed to address the concern, and may remove content, issue a warning, or
restrict participation. Participants should respect these decisions and may
request reconsideration privately with relevant context.
